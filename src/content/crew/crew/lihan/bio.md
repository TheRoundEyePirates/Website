# About Me

## Personal History

I'm a robotics enthusiast from South Africa now living in New Zealand, where I've kept building out my love for engineering, programming, electronics, and computer science. Growing up across different countries taught me to adapt fast, pick up new skills quickly, and see problems from fresh angles. Every new environment brought different people and ideas, and those experiences shaped how I collaborate, create, and solve problems today.

## Robotics Journey

Robotics started with curiosity: how do machines actually work, and how can technology solve real problems? It's the rare field where creativity, engineering, and programming all come together to turn an idea into something that moves, reacts, and works.

Since then I've worked across the whole build — robot design, CAD modelling, mechanical assembly, electronics, programming, testing, and iterating on performance. I love chasing down hard problems, finding better designs, and building reliable, competitive robots with a team. Every project leaves me with something new: an engineering trick, a software lesson, or a better way of working together.

## Robotics Goals

My goal is to reach Worlds and compete at the highest level of FTC. I want to keep sharpening my engineering, programming, and teamwork, and help the crew build robots that are innovative, reliable, and genuinely competitive. I'm also keen to learn from teams around the world, swap ideas, and give back to the global robotics community. Robotics is about more than building machines — it's about learning to think creatively, solve problems, work as a team, and shape the technology of the future.
